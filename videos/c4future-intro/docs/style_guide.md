# Style guide — C4Future intro

**Palette (measured, main.css :root):** bg #050810, surface #0d1525, border rgba(255,255,255,.08), text #f0f4ff, secondary #8899bb, accent #64ffb4. Cyan #00d9ff / violet #8b5cf6 appear only where the site itself uses them (logo mark, gradient on "Carbon Cost" headline in screenshots) — never in our own type.
**Type:** Space Grotesk 700, tracking -0.03em, for display lines; Inter 500 for captions/UI. Left-aligned at x ≥ 140 (16:9) / 80 (9:16). Sentence case, full stops as on the site ("Measure & Reduce" style headings use accent on the second phrase).
**Texture:** the site's sparse green dot field — seeded, drifting slowly, 8–20 % alpha. No glow on UI.
**Rhythm:** 120 BPM, bar = 2 s. Hard cuts on bars (b8, b16, b24, b32); montage cuts every 2 beats; inner hits on beats.
**Transitions:** hard cuts and push-throughs only. No crossfades.
**Camera:** every held screenshot drifts 1.00 → 1.05; UI cards settle from slight skew + scale (fake 2D depth) with deep soft shadow.
**Text in/out:** masked word rise (heavy for display, snappy for captions); exits lift through the mask.
**Sound:** pops on words, ticks on typing, click on Calculate, shutter on cuts, riser → thump on logo.
